Our Story

About Us

"We don't press play. We create moments people carry with them for the rest of their lives."

Emerald City Sound & Events was born out of a simple belief: the music at your event isn't background — it's the heartbeat of everything that happens. It drives the energy in the room, pulls people onto the dance floor, and creates the moments you'll remember forever.

With over 15 years of experience and more than 400 events produced across the Pacific Northwest, we've refined our approach into something that goes far beyond just DJing. We're event partners — collaborating with you from the planning stages through to the final song of the night.

Our roots are deep in Seattle's music and entertainment scene. We've worked everything from intimate backyard weddings in Kirkland to major corporate galas in downtown Seattle, from Microsoft team events to late-night club sets — and we bring the same level of preparation and passion to every single one.

What drives us is simple: seeing a dance floor packed, watching people's faces light up during a first dance, or hearing the roar of a crowd when we drop exactly the right song at exactly the right moment. That's why we do this.
